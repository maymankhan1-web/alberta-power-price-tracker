**Tier name:** Alberta Power Price Alerts
**Suggested price:** $3/month (Mohammad to decide)

**Description:**
Get notified when Alberta's wholesale power pool price spikes or crashes. Every hour, this tracker checks AESO's public data. When the price goes above $200/MWh, falls below $10/MWh, or jumps $100/MWh in an hour, sponsors at this tier get an alert. Useful for anyone on a floating-rate plan, running flexible loads, or just watching the market.

- Hourly checks from official AESO data
- Spike, drop and big-jump alerts
- Ask for custom thresholds
- Supports the free public tracker

Data comes from AESO (Alberta Electric System Operator). Not affiliated with AESO. Not financial or energy-purchasing advice.
